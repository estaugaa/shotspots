How to shoot it

Inside is the interesting part: the marble rotunda at opening, with the Gorge through the windows.

A sunrise spot, best any month. It's roadside, so it works on short notice when conditions line up.

Landscape: From the parking lot at dusk, light trails curve around the building. Layers sell scale, so give the frame three to five overlapping ridgelines and compress them at 150–300 mm. Haze is your friend for layering, not your enemy.

Birds & wildlife: Ravens play in the updrafts here in strong east wind; they'll hang at eye level.

Open to both sunrise and sunset.

Starter spot From OpenStreetMap and Wikidata. The tip is general and the pin isn't checked yet; ShotSpots researches the most notable starter spots first. Scores, light and drive times work the same as for any spot.

How to shoot it

Works at sunrise or sunset, best any month. It's a real hike: commit only when the forecast is solid and allow time to get there before the light.

Landscape: At sunrise the alpenglow on the peak comes 10–15 minutes before the sun reaches you, so be set up and composed by then. Watch the peak's shadow side too: pink snow next to blue shadow is the color contrast. Arrive 45 minutes early and shoot blue hour too: the river holds color after the sky goes flat.
